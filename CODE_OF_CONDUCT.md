# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct
Hyrax Engine's Code of Conduct is simple and straight forward.
Download the Engine, Feel free to modify and do whatever you want as long as you adhere to the respected licenses of the O3DE Engine when it comes to using it.
## Hyrax Pledge
Hyrax Engine excists to ensure everyone who wants to build games can no matter what political nature they lean to and to ensure Game Development and production in the Open AAA, AA and A spaces can continue without interruption to users of HyraxEngine feel good and safe using the Hyrax Engine.

Hyrax Engine must ensure Users can use it in the ways they see fit and no user should ever risk getting excluded from HyraxEngine because of disagreement with certain political groups.

Hyrax Engine must at all times be kept free of Drama and Politics in all ways as it is just a tool to create and should always be treated as such.

Users must be free to distribute and share works created with it in any way and must not face restrictions unless those restrictions are due to the Licenses of certain components such as the GNU-GPL, Apache 2.0 or MIT licenses but as long as these licenses are adheered to, Users of Hyrax Engine can use it in any ways they see fit and should be encorraged to modify and share things with other users.

## Our Standards
Avoid Politics
Avoid arguements related stances held by groups or individuals
Focus on making stuff and make that stuff work.
Prioritize making games and software that users enjoy using or playing.
Understand Hyrax Engine is a tool based on O3DE but is not associated with O3DE, Hyrax Engine is a fork of the O3DE engine and should not involve itself in anything but, "Use This if you wanna make stuff"


## Enforcement Responsibilities
We do not have those, that's why we're a fork right?

## Scope
Hyrax Engine must not be Vibe Coded, Hyrax Engine must be used to make cool stuff
