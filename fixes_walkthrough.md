- **1.4 Compression buffer overflow:** Clamped compression destination capacity to buffer capacity and fell back to uncompressed sends in [TcpConnection.cpp](/o3de/Code/Framework/AzNetworking/AzNetworking/TcpTransport/TcpConnection.cpp) and [UdpNetworkInterface.cpp](/o3de/Code/Framework/AzNetworking/AzNetworking/UdpTransport/UdpNetworkInterface.cpp).

- **1.7 TLS connection leak:** Added a private transfer constructor in [TlsSocket.h](/o3de/Code/Framework/AzNetworking/AzNetworking/TcpTransport/TlsSocket.h) / [TlsSocket.cpp](/o3de/Code/Framework/AzNetworking/AzNetworking/TcpTransport/TlsSocket.cpp) so `CloneAndTakeOwnership` no longer allocates and leaks a redundant `SSL_CTX*`/`SSL*`.
